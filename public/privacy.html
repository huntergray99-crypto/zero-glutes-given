Privacy Policy

Zero Glutes Given · Last updated October 26, 2026

Zero Glutes Given is a map of celiac-safe and gluten-free restaurants. This policy explains what the app collects and what it doesn't. It's short because the app does very little with your data.

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can use the entire map without an account.
  • Signing in saves your reviews, check-in history, posts and comments to the cloud, and adds a shared feed and a points leaderboard.
  • Sending a report or a spot suggestion also leaves your device, whether or not you are signed in.
  • Your location, when you turn it on, is used live on your device. The coordinates are never stored or transmitted — a check-in records only the restaurant, the time, and whether your device confirmed you were nearby.
  • You can delete your account and everything tied to it from inside the app.

Stored on your device only

The app keeps the following in your browser's local storage and never transmits it:

  • Reviews, draft posts, and check-ins you make while signed out
  • Your chosen neighborhood, map style, and other preferences
  • Photos you attach to posts (held in your browser; not uploaded)

Clearing your browser or app storage erases all of it.

Stored in the cloud (only if you sign in)

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication by Google LLC. You may sign in with Google or continue as a guest, which creates an anonymous ID with no personal details.

If you sign in, the following is saved to Firebase Cloud Firestore so other users can see the shared social layer:

  • A display handle you choose (you do not have to use your real name)
  • Posts and comments you write — with the restaurant they refer to and a timestamp
  • Reviews you write (rating, whether you were glutened, and your text), shown publicly next to your handle
  • Your check-in history: which restaurants you checked in at, when, and whether your device confirmed you were nearby (a yes/no flag — never your coordinates). Only you can read it; other users see only your totals.
  • Your points, level, and activity counts (check-ins, verified check-ins, spots, reviews, posts, badges), shown on the public leaderboard card
  • The account ID Firebase issues

If you sign in with Google, Google provides the app your name, email address, and profile photo per your Google Account settings. These are used to create your account and are not shown publicly unless you place them in your handle.

Reports and spot suggestions

When you use "report a change" or "suggest a spot," what you write is saved to Firebase Cloud Firestore with the restaurant it refers to and a timestamp — and, if you are signed in, your handle and account ID. You do not need to be signed in to send one. Reports are not public; only you and the app's operators can read them.

When a report arrives, a notification containing the restaurant name, your text, and your handle (if any) may be sent to a private moderation channel on Discord (Discord Inc.) so a person can review it quickly. Please don't put personal information in a report.

Location

If you tap "Use my location," the app reads your device's GPS position to center the map, pick your nearest neighborhood, and confirm you are at a restaurant for a check-in bonus. This happens entirely on your device in real time. Your coordinates are not saved, logged, or sent to any server — not ours, not Firebase. A check-in saves the restaurant, the time, and a yes/no flag for whether your device confirmed you were there. You can turn location off at any time; the app works without it.

Other services the app touches

  • Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap, MapTiler, Esri, and OpenFreeMap. As with any website, requesting a map tile reveals your IP address and the area you are viewing to those providers.
  • The app is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), whose servers log IP addresses for standard security and operations.
  • Delivery and ride links (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Uber) open those companies' own sites or apps. Once you leave Zero Glutes Given, their privacy policies apply. These links may pass through an affiliate redirect that records the click.
  • Premium purchases, if and when they are offered, are handled by Stripe (Stripe, Inc.) on its own checkout page. Stripe receives your account ID and email address to complete the purchase. We learn whether it succeeded; we never see your card details.

Deleting your account and data

To delete your Zero Glutes Given account and the data tied to it:

  1. Open the app and tap your points badge at the top to open Your card.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete everything. If you signed in with Google, you'll be asked to confirm that Google account first.

This immediately and permanently removes your posts, comments, reviews, check-in history, reports, leaderboard card, and your sign-in. It cannot be undone.

Can't open the app, or signed in as a guest on a device you no longer have? Email the address below, tell us your handle, and we will delete it for you.

  • Device data: you control it — clear it in your browser or OS settings. Deleting your account does not erase what is saved only on your device.
  • Kept after deletion: a record of any Premium purchase, as needed for tax, accounting, and refunds. A moderation notification already delivered to Discord is not recalled automatically; we will remove it on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Material changes will update the "last updated" date above.
