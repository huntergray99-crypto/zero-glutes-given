**Full description** (4000 char max)
```
Zero Glutes Given is a map for people who can't just "try a little" gluten.

Every spot is rated by how safe it actually is for celiacs — from dedicated
gluten-free kitchens to places with a shared fryer you should know about before
you order. No guessing, no digging through reviews.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• A map and browse view of celiac-safe and gluten-free spots across Seattle and
  the Eastside
• Safety levels on every listing: dedicated GF kitchen, dedicated fryer,
  celiac-verified, or gluten-friendly
• "What to order" notes and the details that matter — cross-contamination
  practices, separate prep, staff awareness
• Filter by neighborhood, cuisine, or distance
• Getting-there notes: parking, transit, and bike info for each spot
• Delivery and pickup links, plus late-night options for night owls

MAKE IT YOURS
• Check in to spots you've visited and earn points as you go
• Leave celiac-focused reviews and community notes for other diners
• Suggest a new spot or report a change when something's out of date
• Sign in to share a feed with other GF diners, or use it solo — your call

Built by someone who eats gluten-free and got tired of calling restaurants
ahead. Free, no ads.

Coverage is Seattle-focused today and growing. Spot missing? Tell us in the app.
```
